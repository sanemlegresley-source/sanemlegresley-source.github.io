Case study · Member digital experience

Modernising the member digital experience

How I would lead the digital experience for a public-sector pension plan: a website modernisation and a member engagement strategy that make a complex benefit feel simple. Task-based architecture, plain-language forms and letters, accessibility built in, a design system that scales quality, the responsible use of AI, all delivered in waves with the team carried through the change. A worked, illustrative case for a public-sector pension context. The approach is the real thing.

Engagement strategy Website modernisation Forms & correspondence WCAG 2.2 AA Design system Responsible AI Change management

01 · The mandate

A simple experience for a genuinely complex benefit

The strategy: a digital-first, member-centred experience across every channel. Members should be able to understand their pension and complete the task they came for without a phone call and employers should be able to self-serve with confidence.

My frame: modernisation is not a redesign of pages. It is a redesign of tasks, content and letters around what members actually need, governed so quality holds as the site and the plans evolve.

What a quick audit tends to surface

  • • Members call because the site answers "what is it" but not "what do I do".
  • • Forms and letters are written in plan language, not plain language.
  • • Self-service exists but stalls partway through the hardest tasks.
  • • Accessibility is tested at the end, not designed in from the start.
  • • Content lives in many templates with no single source of truth.

Decisions follow research and data, not opinion

Before anything is designed, the work is grounded: top member tasks, why members call, where self-service drops off and what the analytics and usability testing say. The modernisation is prioritised against that evidence.

04 · Task-based architecture

Organise around tasks, not the org chart

A pension site drifts toward naming itself after internal departments. Members think in tasks. The modernised IA leads with them and puts the secure, personalised work behind one clear sign-in.

  • Home (leads with the top member tasks)
  • ├─ Plan my retirement (estimate, options, apply)
  • ├─ Understand my pension (plain-language guides)
  • ├─ Life changes (new job, leaving, marriage, death)
  • ├─ Forms & documents (structured, accessible)
  • └─ My Account (secure)
    • ├─ Estimate & apply
    • ├─ Update my details
    • └─ My letters & documents

05 · Forms & correspondence

Letters a member can read once and understand

Correspondence and forms are where trust is won or lost. I treat them as structured content: plain language, a clear next step, accessible by default, authored once and reused, so a change to policy updates every letter cleanly.

Before

"Further to your recent correspondence regarding the commencement of your pension entitlement, please be advised that additional documentation is required in order for the Corporation to adjudicate your request."

After

"We need one more document to start your pension. Please send us your birth certificate by 15 March. Here is how and here is what happens next."

Structured content means the plain-language rewrite, the reading level, the accessibility and the policy accuracy are governed in one place, not re-litigated letter by letter.

06 · Accessibility

Accessible by default, not by audit

For a public service, accessibility is an obligation and a design principle. I build it in and govern it, rather than testing for it at the end and hoping.

Designed in

  • WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline for web, portals, forms and documents.
  • Plain language and a readability target on every member-facing page.
  • Keyboard, screen-reader and reflow checks in the component itself.
  • Accessible PDFs and letters, not just accessible web pages.

Governed

  • Accessibility acceptance criteria on every story, checked before release.
  • A conformance measure reported alongside delivery, not buried.
  • A clear channel for members to request an accommodation, honoured quickly.
  • Regular audits treated as defects to fix, with owners and dates.

07 · Design system & governance

Quality that scales because it is built once

A design system and a content model are how a small team keeps a large estate consistent and accessible. Components carry their accessibility and their plain-language patterns with them, so every new page starts compliant.

Design system

Accessible, tested components and tokens. One button, one form field, one set of states, reused across web and portals.

Content model

Structured, reusable content for guides, forms and letters, authored once and published everywhere, with plain-language standards baked in.

Governance

Clear standards, review and quality gates, so consistency and accessibility survive growth and staff change.

08 · Responsible AI & automation

AI where it helps a member, with guardrails

AI earns a place when it makes a member's task easier and a team's work lighter, never at the cost of accuracy or trust in a regulated benefit.

Member-facing: a grounded assistant that answers pension questions from approved, plain-language content and policy, cites what it is based on and hands off to a person for anything it is not confident about or that touches a member's specific entitlement.

Behind the scenes: drafting and triage that free staff for the complex, human work: proposing plain-language rewrites for review, summarising a member's history for the contact centre and routing correspondence. Always reviewed, always governed, never the final word on a benefit.

09 · Delivered in waves

Phased delivery, with the team carried through it

Modernisation this size succeeds in waves that each ship real value, not a single risky launch. Each wave is paired with change management so staff, partners and members adopt it.

Wave 1 · Foundations

  • • Design system and content model v1
  • • Top member tasks, rebuilt and accessible
  • • Plain-language standard for forms and letters

Wave 2 · Self-service

  • • Estimate and apply in My Account
  • • Employer Portal improvements
  • • Correspondence modernised and automated

Wave 3 · Personalised

  • • New plan websites, fully modernised
  • • Grounded member assistant
  • • Proactive, personalised guidance
Change management: every wave ships with enablement for staff, clear communication to members and employers, partner and vendor alignment and a read on adoption. The reliability of today's service is protected while tomorrow's is built.

11 · Leading the function

How I lead a digital experience team

Empower the experts

I lead managers and professional staff across digital products, UX, content, education, forms and correspondence by setting clear direction and priorities, then creating the conditions for them to do their best work.

Balance ambition and reliability

Decide on evidence

I set performance measures, read the quantitative and qualitative evidence together and make sound calls in a complex, changing environment, with partners and vendors aligned behind them.
