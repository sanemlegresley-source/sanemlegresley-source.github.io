01 · Read the organisation

Whitespace hides between stakeholders

A pharma brand is not one client. It is medical, patient engagement, marketing and commercial, each with its own mandate, budget and MLR reality. The best organic growth solves for two of them at once, in a way none of them would have scoped alone.

06 · Win the room

The narrative I would bring to the SVP

The challenge, reframed

"You asked for a campaign refresh. The data says your real problem is persistence: a third of patients lapse in the first 90 days and that is where the revenue is leaking."

The insight

"The moment that matters is onboarding, not acquisition. Patients and caregivers make the stay-or-lapse decision early and no one is designing for it across medical and commercial together."

The ask

"Here is a phased program, the stakeholders it aligns, an MLR-efficient content model and the revenue it protects. Start with a pilot, prove the lift, scale from there."

Landscape read: patient centricity is now a commercial mandate, not a nice-to-have. Access is uneven, caregivers are underserved, MLR is the real bottleneck to speed and measurement is the currency that earns the next budget. A partner who names those dynamics before the client does becomes a trusted advisor, not a vendor.

07 · Run it like a pipeline

Discipline behind the story

Organic growth is a pipeline, not a hope. Each opportunity moves through stages with a probability, a next step and a date, kept honest in the CRM and reported to leadership.

Identified → Qualified (need confirmed) → POV shared → Proposed → Won, incremental revenue

Weighted pipeline feeds the forecast. Stalled opportunities get a reason and an owner. Nothing sits in "identified" forever.
