A few things I believe about this work

It's one loop, shared

Acquisition, onboarding, support and retention belong to different teams but move together. Instrumenting the whole loop tends to be a collaboration, not a solo act.

Automation needs guardrails

A useful automation comes with a human-in-the-loop checkpoint, a way to measure whether it's working, a rollback, and a clear owner. Adoption and trust matter more than cleverness.

Let the test decide

Where it's practical, an experiment settles a debate better than seniority does — and a well-run one is honest about uncertainty rather than hiding it.
