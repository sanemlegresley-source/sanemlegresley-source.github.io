Automation opportunity mapping

A simple way to decide what to automate first. Each recurring process is scored on effort and impact; the ones worth doing land on a phased plan with the hours they'd give back. It's how a team can turn "we should use AI" into a short, honest list instead of a wish.

Staged rollout

Sequenced in three phases — often runnable in weeks rather than quarters when the early wins are low-risk.

Phase 1 · Prove it

  • • Email triage & routing (live)
  • • Client intake & onboarding forms
  • • Meeting notes → action items

Lowest risk · fastest to value

Phase 2 · Scale it

  • • Proposal & quote drafting
  • • Invoice reconciliation
  • • Weekly reporting rollups

Needs system integrations

Phase 3 · Compound it

  • • Knowledge-base assistant
  • • Churn-signal monitoring
  • • Forecast & capacity planning

Highest leverage

Governance: each automation ships with a human-in-the-loop checkpoint, a rollback path, an accuracy metric, and a named owner. Change management is treated as part of the build, not an afterthought — adoption is the ROI.
