How the approach fits together

Score

A weighted health score per account — usage depth, feature adoption, NPS/sentiment, support burden, and on-time payment — recomputed regularly so a decline shows up as a trend, not a surprise.

Signal

Thresholds trigger plays: a sustained usage drop opens a check-in; a negative ticket streak routes to the account owner; an approaching renewal on a red account gets a senior touch well before the date.

Learn

Interventions get logged with their outcome, so over time the team can see which plays actually help which kinds of accounts — and lean on evidence rather than instinct.
