Reading the evidence, not just the dashboard

Numbers set the direction. The judgement is in reading them together with what members say and do.

Quant and qual together

A rising completion rate with falling satisfaction is a warning, not a win. I read the two together before I act.

Equity in the numbers

A good average can hide a group the service is failing. I check that accessibility and task success hold across audiences, not just on average.

Measures drive the roadmap

The metric that is stuck is the next thing the team works on. Performance measurement is how priorities get set, not just reported.
