The way I like to approach it

Measure honestly

Each channel carries its fully-loaded CAC and a payback period, so the comparison is like-for-like and a click that never becomes revenue doesn't get credit for the ones that do.

Reallocate steadily

Budget drifts toward the channels clearing the ROAS floor and away from those that don't — ideally validated with a proper test rather than a hunch. Cheap creative iteration keeps the top of funnel fed.

Close the loop

Handing client success a scored, well-fit lead — not just a signup — lets acquisition and retention be tuned together, which tends to work better than optimising either team in isolation.
